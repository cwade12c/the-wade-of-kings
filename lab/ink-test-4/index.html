Starfall

The climax of Chapter 19 in full 3D: the hillside, the beasts closing in, the blue light falling, and what stands up out of it. About 20 seconds, looping.

loading…
  • From the page: the beasts close in on Dalinar, who curls around Seeli against a flat rock face while Taffa's lamp oil burns out on the stones. A brilliant blue light falls like a star rock and cracks the stone, and the beasts freeze. It stands up: a man in glowing blue Shardplate, Stormlight trailing from him. The beasts throw themselves at him, and his Blade shears them in half, the pieces trailing black smoke. Other lights fall toward the village [ch 19].
  • My staging: the hillside's shape, where everyone stands, the camera, and how each moment is timed. The knight is drawn as Plate and light, without a face, so nothing is designed that the page hasn't shown.
  • Everything is generated: the terrain and rock detail come from code, the beasts and figures were built and rigged in Blender, and the light, smoke, dust and glow are real-time effects.
