The poker thrust, in lamplit ink

The same ten seconds from Chapter 19, rebuilt: a beast sculpted and rigged in Blender, the farmhouse built to the page, and one look that combines the two from the first test. Darkness is ink; wherever light falls, the ink turns back into paper.

  • The beast is one sculpted body, rigged with 27 bones, so the neck coils, the head weaves, the legs step and the body empties out. The page gives six thin legs bent at the sides, a boneless neck, no eyes, black teeth and skin that reflects light like tar [ch 19]. The rest of its form is my staging.
  • The farmhouse is built from the page: a stone floor, crem-brick walls, a fireless brick hearth with a rough iron pot hanging over it, a low table and a pair of stools, and a glassless window with a broken shutter [ch 19]. The rafters and the floor's flagstones are my staging.
  • The light is Taffa's oil lamp and Salas's violet moonlight through the window. The ink look is drawn from it every frame.
  • Dalinar is still out of shot on purpose; the poker comes in from beyond the frame.
