A living painting

Your farmhouse painting, brought to life: real depth for the camera to move through, the painted smoke rising and curling out of the wound, the candle breathing light into the room, and the beasts never quite still.

  • Depth: a depth-estimation model read the painting, so the camera's slow push and drift give true parallax: the table and candle slide past Heb, who slides past the hearth wall. "Depth map" shows what it saw.
  • Smoke: the smoke is the painting's own brushwork, made to flow upward and curl, and it surges when the camera jolts.
  • Light and life: the candle flame sways and its light breathes across the room; dust drifts through it. The beast's neck and the one at the window weave a little; Taffa and Seeli tremble.
