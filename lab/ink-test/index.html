The poker thrust, in 3D

Ten seconds from the farmhouse in Chapter 19, built in three.js to compare two looks before the full dispatch. Watch it on your phone too, and tell me which look, and how smooth it ran.

  • A · Lamplight: lit like film. Taffa's oil lamp is the main light, and Salas's violet moonlight comes through the broken window behind the beast.
  • B · Ink painting: the same scene, the same light, re-drawn every frame as ink washes and brush outlines on paper.
  • In both, the beast is one liquid surface that flows as it moves, with a glossy skin, and the smoke is a GPU particle jet. The page gives six thin legs, no eyes, black teeth, skin that reflects light like tar, and a wound that bleeds smoke [ch 19]. The rest is my staging.
  • Dalinar is only an arm and the poker here, on purpose: a test of the medium, not of people. The full piece would use real motion capture for him.
