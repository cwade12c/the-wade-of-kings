      How it was made

      Everything is drawn live in your browser with three.js (MIT licence): the chasm, the spear, the water and the light. The spear’s path through the whole kata is laid down once as brush strokes; each stroke lights as the spear passes, keeps the colour of the memory it was painted in, then dries and fades. The memories are ink studies, revealed where the spear’s sweeps cross them and spreading outward from there like ink on wet paper. In the wind, thousands of streaks of water ride a vortex around the kata on the same clock, which is why the last snap can stop them in the air, and the pool reflects everything that gives light. Every frame is worked out from the film’s clock alone, so you can scrub to any instant, switch views, or pause and walk around a held moment. On a phone it draws a lighter version and lowers its resolution if frames run long.
