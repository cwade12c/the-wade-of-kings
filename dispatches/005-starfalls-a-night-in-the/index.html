    How it was made

    • Fourteen paintings, one for each beat of the night, in the order the page tells it.
    • Each painting was created from the pages: six thin legs bent at the sides, no eyes, black teeth, skin that shines like tar, wounds that bleed smoke; a fireless hearth, a glassless window, crem bricks; one small violet moon. Every image was checked against the chapter before it was kept, and the ones that strayed were made again.
    • Faces the book hasn't described aren't drawn: figures are seen from behind, in shadow, or helmed.
    • A depth model read each painting, so the camera moves through it with real parallax. The painted smoke flows, flames breathe light, necks weave, and dust, embers and Stormlight drift over the top.
    • The words over each scene are the book's own, a sentence or two from the moment it shows, and so are the closing words.
