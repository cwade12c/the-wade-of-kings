    Lit, glowing spheres are viewpoint characters; squares are groups. In The Cast, a group's members share a dotted territory, each pair shares one line, spheres grow with how often the book has named someone, and more names appear as you zoom in. Arrows mark command, dots support, bars hostility; faint gold lines are family ties that follow from the others. Pick anyone, or find them by name, to see all their ties.

    Lenses light some names and fade the rest, and nobody moves: this chapter's cast, everyone on stage in the chapters told through one viewpoint's eyes, a family or crew, or whoever Wade's open questions and predictions are about. Picking anyone still shows all their ties; under This chapter, the ones the chapter doesn’t show fade, and those they’re on the page with are labelled with what passed between them. From a name's card, trace the chain of ties to anyone else.
