    Lit, glowing spheres are viewpoint characters; squares are groups. In The Cast, a group's members share a dotted territory, spheres grow with how often the book has named someone, lines stay quiet until you pick someone, and more names appear as you zoom in. Arrows mark command, dots support, bars hostility; faint gold lines are family ties that follow from the others. Pick anyone, or find them by name, to see all their ties.
